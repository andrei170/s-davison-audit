Why this is the same job as the tender work

A selection questionnaire asks for named contracts, values, dates, case studies, references and accreditations. Every one of those is also missing from the site.

So writing them once serves both. You are not choosing between a website and getting on approved lists — the website is the evidence pack, in a form a buyer can read before they ring.

Which one we would actually take, and why

Option B, for one reason: the tender credentials page. Getting on an approved-contractor list at Newcastle or Sunderland means producing that evidence pack on request. Having it already written and hosted is worth more than the £500 over one submission.

The photo area matters too, and it was your idea, not ours. You said people want to see what you do and that industrial work is not something most electricians can show. Option A cannot show it; Option B can, and you can keep adding to it without paying anybody.

But Option A is not a bad buy. If you would rather open the door, get the credentials up and stop there, it does that job completely.
