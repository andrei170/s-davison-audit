How Go-Live Works
  • 1. You approve the draft in writing. A reply to an email is enough.
  • 2. We build the live version: the draft bar comes off and Google is allowed to list it.
  • 3. A 15-minute screen share for GoDaddy, because the login code goes to your phone. Only the website records change. Your email records are not touched.
  • 4. We send a test email each way to prove your email still works.
  • 5. We submit the sitemap to Google and check your analytics is recording.
  • While you are away: tell us who can approve changes and receive the GoDaddy code, if you want it live before you are back.
